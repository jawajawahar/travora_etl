# Emission factor sources

Downloaded 2026-09-26. Values below were read from these files directly.

| File | Source | Values used |
|---|---|---|
| 01, 02, 03 | UK Department for Energy Security and Net Zero (DESNZ), *Greenhouse gas reporting: conversion factors 2024*, v1.1. https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/greenhouse-gas-reporting-conversion-factors-2024 | Sheet "Factors by Category", Level 1 "Business travel- land", kg CO2e: national rail 0.03546 /passenger-km; average local bus 0.10846 /pkm; coach 0.02717 /pkm; regular taxi 0.14861 /pkm; average car (unknown fuel) 0.16691 /vehicle-km. Level 1 "Fuels": petrol (average biofuel blend) 2.0844 kg CO2e/litre |
| 04 | DESNZ, *2024 Government GHG Conversion Factors for Company Reporting: Methodology Paper* | How the factors above are derived; scope (direct, tank-to-wheel) |
| 05 | India GHG Program, *India Specific Rail Transport Emission Factors for Passenger Travel and Material Transport*, 2015, v1.0 (stakeholder consultation). https://indiaghgp.org | p.17: passenger rail 0.00794 kg CO2/pax-km (non-suburban), 2012-13 Indian Railways data, mixed diesel and electric traction |
| 06 | India GHG Program / WRI India, *India Specific Road Transport Emission Factors*, 2015, v1.0 (stakeholder consultation) | p.11: three-wheeler, petrol 0.1135 kg CO2/vehicle-km; p.12: bus 0.015161 kg CO2/pax-km |
| 07 | India GHG Program, road transport emission factors web page | Landing page for 06 |
| 08, 08b | Konara, Samarasekara, Chaminda, Perera (2019). Comparison of Emission Levels of Passenger Transport Modes in Sri Lanka. *Journal of Atmospheric Pollution* 7(1). doi:10.12691/jap-7-1-2 | Not used for factors: reports exhaust concentration per seat (% v/v), not g CO2 per km. Cited for Sri Lankan context only |
| 09 | Surrey County Council, *Carbon emissions per passenger kilometre* (uses BEIS 2021 factors) | Older edition, comparison only |

Notes for the methods chapter:
- DESNZ factors are CO2e; India factors are CO2 only (difference about 1-2%).
- All factors are direct (tank-to-wheel); upstream fuel production is excluded.
- No official Sri Lanka-specific per passenger-km factors were found; India (South Asian operating conditions) and UK factors bound the sensitivity range.
- Both India documents are marked "for stakeholder consultation, version 1.0".
